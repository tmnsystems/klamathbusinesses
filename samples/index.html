Sample pages

Tina's Local Business Packages, Klamath Falls. Four one page samples, one for each kind of business we serve.

Contractors

Services, service area, photos of past work, license information, and a tap to call button.

Food, venues, hospitality

Menu, hours, location, events, and phone. Lodging businesses also get a book direct section.

Professional services

Services, credentials, office details, and a contact form.

Retail

Hours, address, what the store carries, and phone, with an optional appointment and order section.

How to read these samples

Every bracket on a sample page is a slot. When a real site is built, the slots are filled with that business's own information. Each page also includes a chat helper that answers customer questions from the business's own details. These are samples, not live sites.
